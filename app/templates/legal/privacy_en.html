Privacy Policy

This document is a template prepared for the AI Job Copilot service. The operator must complete the controller details and have it reviewed by a legal professional before public launch.

1. Controller

[Company name, address, email]. Data protection contact: {{ support }}.

2. What data we process

  • Telegram account data: user ID, username, first name, interface language.
  • CV data you provide: uploaded files, contact details, work history, education, skills, languages, portfolio links.
  • Job search preferences: target roles, locations, work format, salary expectations, exclusions, work authorisation you state.
  • Generated content: CV versions, cover letters, application history and statuses.
  • Email connection: Gmail address and an encrypted OAuth refresh token (only if you connect Gmail).
  • Payment records: product, amount, status, Telegram payment identifiers (no card data).

3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Providing the service you request — CV creation, job matching, cover letters, sending applications (contract, Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Processing CV data with AI providers — based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Security, abuse prevention and audit logs (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Accounting for payments (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not use age, gender, nationality or other protected characteristics to rank vacancies.

4. Recipients

  • AI providers (e.g. OpenAI or Google) to analyse CVs and job descriptions and generate texts.
  • Hosting, database and file storage providers.
  • Google (Gmail API) — only to send emails you explicitly confirm.
  • Employers — only the application you confirm before sending.
  • Telegram — as the messaging and payment platform.

Transfers outside the EEA rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

5. Retention

Data is stored while your account is active. You can delete individual CVs or your entire account in the bot (Settings → Privacy & data). Accounts inactive for 24 months are deleted. Anonymised audit entries and payment records required by law are kept for the statutory period.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. Export and deletion are available directly in the bot. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

7. Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, connections use HTTPS, access to personal data is restricted and logged, and CV content is never written to application logs.

8. Gmail data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Sending requires only the gmail.send scope. Reading replies is optional and requested separately.
